Understanding Market Structure & Technical Chart Indicators

Published on October 10, 2026 | Category: Quantitative Analysis

Quantitative financial analysis involves examining historical price data, volume patterns, and momentum indicators to evaluate market behavior. Utilizing platforms like TradingView allows participants to backtest paper trading strategies systematically.

1. Moving Averages and Trend Identification

Simple Moving Averages (SMA) and Exponential Moving Averages (EMA) help smooth out price action noise to identify primary market trends over specific timeframes, filtering out short-term fluctuations.

2. Volume Weight and Breakout Confirmation

Volume is a critical verification metric. A price breakout accompanied by high relative volume indicates strong institutional participation, whereas low volume breakouts often signal false movements.
