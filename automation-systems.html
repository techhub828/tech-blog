Logic and Automation: Implementing Float Sensors & Motor Controllers

Published on October 8, 2026 | Category: Automation & Hardware

Electromechanical automation simplifies daily infrastructure management. By integrating basic sensor feedback loops with motor control panels, fluid storage systems can operate entirely autonomously without manual oversight.

1. How Float Switches Work

Float switches use a weighted internal mechanism or magnetic reed switch that changes state depending on liquid levels. Placed inside underground tanks and overhead reservoirs, they act as binary triggers signaling when liquid is low or full.

2. Preventing Dry Runs and Overflow

Automatic water level controllers combine multi-point sensors with relay switches to protect pump motors from burning out due to dry running, while automatically shutting off pumps once overhead tanks reach capacity.
